## Cinematography & Sound Bible
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1.85:1 Spherical 35mm
- **Cameras & Lenses:** Arriflex 35BL4 / Panavision Panaflex with Cooke & Zeiss High Speed Primes (27mm, 35mm, 50mm); authentic Eastman 5296 tungsten film grain
- **Lighting Arc:** Opens in a glowing, steam-hazed restaurant kitchen in unbroken Steadicam motion, sweeping out into lush Copacabana crimson-velvet tungsten light, shaded table lamps, cigarette haze, and clinking crystal; freezes abruptly into cold, breathless booth tension under single warm overhead spotlight during 'Funny how?'.
- **Color Grade:** Michael Ballhaus lush ruby reds, rich mahogany shadows, golden amber skin warmth, crisp tuxedo ivory highlights.
- **Score & Sound:** Classic 1960s girl-group / doo-wop needle-drop cutting dead silent on whip-pan, kitchen pan clatter, scotch ice clinks, booth leather squeaks, and 'Layla' piano exit swell.
