8-Stage End-to-End Filmmaking Pipeline

Stage 1 — Reference Scene Breakdown & Beat Extraction

  • Inspect source scene cut-by-cut and extract 250–350+ clean reference frames from the original film (refs/yt/<slug>/) to match camera blocking, focal lengths, cyan-dark vs clinical fluorescent lighting transitions, and shot pacing.
  • Build a structured 40-shot shot list (scripts/<slug>_script.json) preserving exact emotional beats, two-shot spatial continuity (left/right eye-line), and scene transitions.

Stage 1.5 — Automated YouTube Cinematography & Optical-Flow Analysis

  • Do not prompt camera movement blindly. Run scripts/analyzeytcamera.py on the extracted source YouTube clip before writing any video generation prompt or keyframe brief:
  • Shot Boundary & RANSAC Optical-Flow Decomposition: Detect exact cuts via PySceneDetect (ContentDetector) and run Lucas-Kanade optical flow (goodFeaturesToTrack + calcOpticalFlowPyrLK + estimateAffinePartial2D RANSAC) at 8 fps inside each cut so actor lip/head motion is ignored as outliers and true global camera motion is isolated cleanly.
  • Measured Camera Motion Telemetry: Compute zoomratepcts ((scale - 1) * 100 / dur), panpcts, tiltpcts, high-frequency handheldjitterpct (RMS residual after subtracting linear pan/tilt), and peakshake_pct.
  • Automatic Motion Tags & Model Routing:
  • IMPACTSHAKE (peakshake >= 1.8%) -> Route to LTX-2.5 + IC-LoRA Cameraman.
  • SLOWDOLLYIN / SLOWDOLLYOUT (|zoom_rate| >= 1.2%/s) -> Route to Lightricks/LTX-2-19b-LoRA-Camera-Control-Dolly-In / Dolly-Out (or LTX-2.5 -> InfiniteTalk V2V for close-up dialogue).
  • PAN_ORBIT (|pan| >= 2.0%/s or |tilt| >= 1.8%/s) -> Route to Dolly-Left/Right or IC-LoRA Cameraman.
  • HANDHELD35MM (handheldjitter >= 0.14%) -> Route to InfiniteTalk I2V + 1.016x 35mm organic weave or Two-Stage LTX Handheld -> InfiniteTalk V2V.
  • STATIC_LOCKED (default) -> Route to InfiniteTalk I2V (dialogue) or LTX-2.5 + Lightricks/LTX-2-19b-LoRA-Camera-Control-Static.
  • Shot Scale, Screen Side & Sequence Grammar Audit: Measure faceheight / frameheight (ECU, CU, MCU, MS, WIDE), lock screen side (left/center/right), and automatically flag consecutive SLOWDOLLYIN streaks (>= 2 in a row or A -> B -> A return-cut push-ins) before preflight passes.

Stage 1.6 — Original YouTube Audio & Music Spotting Analysis

  • Download the source scene's reference audio (yt-dlp -x --audio-format mp3) and audit it natively (read_file) before writing music prompts or SFX timelines:
  • Music Spotting & Texture Fidelity: Record exact timestamps where music is absent (dry room ambience / cold dialogue tension) versus where music enters, and match the real film's harmonic texture (discordant minimalist tension vs. surf-noir needle drop — never slap warm heroic melodies onto dark claustrophobic scenes).
  • Foley Map & Scene Ambience Beds: List every physical sound cue visible or implied (doors, teacups, drills, steps, car cabin hum, impacts) so sound design tracks story beats with peak-safe headroom rather than looping arbitrary SFX over dialogue.

Stage 2 — Nomads-Only Casting & Voice Generation

  • 100% Nomad Cast Rule: Every speaking role is played by a verified Nomad cast member anchored to high-res studio likeness refs — zero generic stock strangers in dialogue shots.
  • Generate clean dialogue stems with ElevenLabs (scripts/gen*audio.py) with authentic vocal delivery (crisp pronunciation, zero trailing gibberish, matched emotional intensity).
  • Inspect waveforms for zero clipping, minimal leading silence, and clean trailing decay; store exact pre-trim MP3 stems in /audio/<slug>/.

Stage 3 — Hero-Anchor Keyframes & Return-Cut Hair/Makeup Locking

  • Generate 21:9 Scope starting (and optional ending) keyframes using Nano Banana Pro (image-generator Ultra subscription first, Paid Tier 3 gemini-3-pro-image at native 21:9 2K as fallback), locking costume, lighting, set dressing, and facial likeness to one single Hero Anchor per character/lighting state.
  • Return-Cut Master Anchor Protocol (Zero Hair Jumps or Mid-Scene Makeup Drift):
  • Establish one Gold-Master Keyframe per recurring camera setup (Setup A, Setup B, etc.) at the start of a lighting act.
  • On EVERY return cut (Shot N+2 returning to the same setup/character as Shot N), pass BOTH the actor's Hero Anchor portrait AND the previously approved keyframe from that setup (Setup A Master or Shot N), explicitly instructing the generator to keep identical hair parting, shoulder/collar hair placement, glasses angle, and prosthetic greasepaint/scar intensity across the sequence.
  • Multi-Face Angle Consistency: When the same character appears in a solo CU and a side-angle group shot, verify ear exposure, hair tuck, and facial makeup density side-by-side before rendering video.
  • Crop and pack every keyframe to exact 1920x832 Panavision Scope (scripts/prep_keyframe.py).
  • Build and visually inspect 5 Pre-Render Cast Continuity Grids across all stills before touching GPU video compute — zero wardrobe swaps, hair flips, glasses changes, or makeup drift allowed to proceed.

Stage 4 — Hybrid Video Rendering (LTX-2.5 + InfiniteTalk on Colab G4 RTX PRO 6000)

  • Single-Anchor Rule on Tight Close-Ups: Omit endimage (endimage = null) on tight talking close-ups so end-frame blending never causes double-exposure dissolves or abrupt tail expression shifts.
  • Technical Camera-Control Architecture (No Default Push-Ins):
  • Positive-Only Physical Camera Directives (LTX-2.5 Rule): LTX-2.5 defaults to a creeping push-in on long clips if camera behavior is unspecified or prompted negatively ("no zoom", "don't move", "no camera movement" confuse the transformer and are blocked by preflightvalidate.py). Always declare positive physical state in a single present-tense paragraph: "static lock-off camera mounted on a heavy tripod, completely stationary framing" for locked coverage, "subtle organic 35mm handheld camera breathing with tiny operator micro-movements, maintaining fixed framing distance" for handheld, or "measured slow linear dolly push-in along fixed axis on weighted rails" strictly when Stage 1.5 detects SLOWDOLLY_IN.
  • Verified Lightricks Camera Control LoRAs & IC-LoRA Cameraman: Load official Lightricks/LTX-2-19b-LoRA-Camera-Control-Static (plus Dolly-In, Dolly-Out, Dolly-Left, Dolly-Right, Jib-Up, Jib-Down) for directional control, or IC-LoRA Cameraman with the extracted source YouTube clip passed as the reference control video at >= 960x512 Stage 1 to transfer genuine cinema handheld shake and panning curves directly.
  • Two-Stage Motion + Lip-Sync (Camera Motion -> InfiniteTalk V2V) & Wan Ecosystem: For shots combining aggressive camera choreography with tight phoneme-locked dialogue and heavy theatrical makeup, generate the clean camera trajectory/body performance first in LTX-2.5 (or Wan2.1/2.2 Uni3C / ReCamMaster / ByteDance ATI trajectory conditioning), then pass that clip through InfiniteTalk V2V (ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper wanvideoInfiniteTalkV2V) so Wav2Vec2 syncs lips cleanly while preserving the exact camera move.
  • LTX-2.5 Dev BF16 (scripts/colabltx25v2.py): Used for Start+End camera choreography and physical action (960x416 24-step euler -> Stage 2 Distilled LoRA 450 x2 latent upscale -> 1920x832). Always freeze custom audio with LTXVFreezeLatent, use zero-latency linear volume scaling (never loudnorm), and never enable LTXVModalityGuidance.
  • InfiniteTalk Wan2.1-14B (scripts/colabinfinitetalkv2.py): Used for phoneme-locked close-up dialogue, tilted/expressive heads, and heavy prosthetic makeup (960x416 dpm++_sde 6-step LightX2V + Wav2Vec2 audio embeddings -> Lanczos/unsharp x2 to 1920x832 muxed with exact clean WAV).
  • Keep GPU alive continuously through full QA, continuity checks, and master screening; never leave stale 00001.mp4 prefixes when re-rendering.

Stage 6 — Post-Render Video Continuity & Adjacent Cut-Pair Audit

  • Run scripts/qavideocontinuity.py on all 40 final rendered MP4s (not source stills):
  • 5 Rendered-Video Character Continuity Grids: Verify facial structure, makeup, lighting contrast, and sharpness across every cut (ensuring LTX-2.5 and InfiniteTalk shots match as one cohesive camera package).
  • Adjacent Cut-Pair Grids (Shot N end next to Shot N+1 start): Verify all 39 edit transitions (01->02 through 39->40) for eye-line continuity, exposure jump, and color temperature lock.

Stage 7 — Hollywood Editorial Assembly, Broadcast Mix & 35mm Grade

  • Tight Nolan Cut Pacing: Detect true last vocal energy (> -38 dB) per clip and trim cleanly to speech_end + 0.20s (0.35s dramatic hold on Act breaks) — zero dead-air frozen tails, zero clipped final consonants.
  • Broadcast Dialogue Leveling (-16 LUFS): Measure per-stem integrated loudness and apply zero-latency linear gain so every cast member speaks at uniform cinema presence, plus 15 ms audio micro-fades at cuts.
  • Cinematic Sound Design (Real Lyria 3.5 Score + Peak-Safe SFX + Continuous Room Tone): Continuous low-passed room tone bed (-42 LUFS, never sliced per cut, <= 5% energy above 2.5 kHz) + Lyria 3.5 musical score (-18 to -19.5 LUFS, >= 35% energy in 250–4,000 Hz, gentle ratio=1.8 ducking under dialogue) + strictly visually-verified peak-normalized SFX cues (normalized by true peak -8 to -14 dBFS, never LUFS-boosted into clipping) + pure linear master gain (-15.0 LUFS) + transparent -2.0 dBFS true-peak limiting (TP <= -1.0 dBTP, LRA >= 2.5 LU, Crest >= 8.0 dB, zero clipped samples).
  • Unified 35 mm Panavision Finishing & Organic Handheld Weave: Color/contrast/sharpness normalization + fine 35 mm film grain + calibrated organic handheld micro-drift (0.35%–0.55% dual-sine weave inside 1.016x overscan on handheld scenes so locked shots breathe instead of freezing) + matte Scope letterbox inside 1920x1080 (< 90 MB, -movflags +faststart web streaming).

Stage 8 — Automated Audio Defect Gate, Hostile QA Subagent & Live Deploy Verification

  • Run python3 scripts/qaaudiov25.py --master <final_mp4> --stems audio/<slug> to enforce midrange musical balance, true-peak headroom (TP <= -1.0 dBTP), uncrushed transients (Crest >= 8.0 dB, LRA >= 2.5 LU), zero clipping, and 220 Hz phone-speaker audibility.
  • Export isolated scoreonly, sfxonly, and finalphone220hz buses plus rendered continuity grids and hand them to an independent Hostile QA Subagent (read-only, zero builder bias) for mandatory per-shot hair/wardrobe/lip-sync sign-off and audio rejection testing before stopping Colab GPUs.
  • Stop Colab GPU only after both automated gates and Hostile QA pass 100%, run python3 scripts/preflightvalidate.py & python3 scripts/buildsite.py, commit cleanly (respecting /clips/, /audio/, .env* ignores), push to GitHub, and verify HTTP 200 on every deployed HTML page AND every referenced .mp4 stream.

Hard-Won Failure Modes Blocked by This Standard

  • Unanchored .gitignore Dropping Web Previews: Never put bare clips/ in .gitignore when hosting web previews under preview/clips/; anchor /clips/ and ALWAYS curl the actual .mp4 URL for HTTP 200.
  • One-Pass loudnorm Desync: FFmpeg's single-pass loudnorm buffers and shifts speech by -30 ms (-2f) + trims starts; always use pre-measured linear volume gain (0 ms shift).
  • LTX-2.5 LTXVModalityGuidance Regression & Joker Grin Prior: Adding LTXVModalityGuidance(3.0) degraded lip sync; LTX-2.5 also defaults to silent Joker smirks mid-shot. Routing complex dialogue/makeup shots to InfiniteTalk solves both cleanly (LSE-C 5.52–7.70).
  • Two-Anchor Morph Dissolves: Feeding endimage into LTX-2.5 on tight CUs causes half-clip double exposures; tight talking CUs must stay single-anchor (endimage = null).
